About us

Learning to govern ourselves, together

Founded in [year], The Self-Governance Institute is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that researches and teaches the habits, skills, and institutions that let people and communities govern themselves. Our aim is to turn the idea of self-rule into practical tools anyone can use at home, at work, and in public life.

Explore our framework
01

Our purpose

We exist to strengthen the capacity of ordinary people to make decisions, hold power accountable, and care for the common good.

02

Our method

We combine research, storytelling, and hands-on training, then test what we learn in real communities.

03

Our values

Responsibility, integrity, and open-minded dialogue, with a commitment to evidence and to the dignity of every person.

A research-backed framework describing the capacities that let individuals and communities govern themselves well.
